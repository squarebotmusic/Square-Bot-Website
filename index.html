Who Is Square Bot

Square Bot

Rooted in Abstract Electronic and Experimental IDM, Square Bot is a boundary-pushing indie artist existing somewhere between man and machine, a half-human, half-robotic presence. Since 2017, Square Bot has served as a limitless creative outlet for the creator behind the helmet, refusing to stay confined within traditional genre lines. More than a music project, Square Bot is an evolving identity where experimentation comes first and creative freedom has no restrictions. The result is music that can shift from one genre to another while still carrying the unmistakable electronic fingerprint that defines Square Bot.
